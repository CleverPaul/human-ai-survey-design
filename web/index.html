About this prototype. Synthetic outcomes, age, and two illustrative groups. Debiasing uses a Bayesian cubic-spline model with group-specific curves. Its intervals depend on the model, prior, and known simulation noise; they are not a guarantee of reliable extrapolation. The optimizer uses profiles, paired differences, and fitted uncertainty.
