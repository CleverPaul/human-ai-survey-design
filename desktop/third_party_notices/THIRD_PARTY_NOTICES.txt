Survey Design Lab - bundled third-party software
================================================

This application uses Python, NumPy, SciPy, PySide6/Shiboken6 and Qt, and PySCIPOpt/SCIP. It is frozen with PyInstaller. Their license texts are included in installed/ and the named component folders. Exact package versions and upstream file hashes are in inventory.json.

Qt and PySide6 are distributed under their applicable open-source licenses, including GNU LGPL v3. QtWebEngine incorporates Chromium. License/copyright texts and attribution metadata from the exact official QtBase and QtWebEngine 6.8.3 source archives are saved in Qt/QtBase-6.8.3-licenses and Qt/QtWebEngine-6.8.3-licenses. Their provenance files record original source paths and SHA-256 hashes. This inclusive source collection also contains build, test, example, and platform-specific components; it does not claim every listed component is linked into this Windows app. GNU GPL and LGPL texts are included in Qt/. The dynamic Qt libraries can be replaced in the one-folder distribution; desktop/ contains instructions to rebuild either distribution from application source. No restriction is imposed here on debugging modifications to these libraries.
